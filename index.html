Morphology Guide

Septate Hyphae (Mold) Filamentous branching tubes containing distinct cross-walls (septa) with pores.
Nonseptate Hyphae (Mold) Continuous, broad filamentous tubes lacking cross-walls.
Budding Yeast Single spherical/oval cells exhibiting budding reproduction.
Pseudohyphae Chains of elongated yeast cells that remain attached after budding, mimicking hyphae.
Cryptococcus neoformans Spherical yeast cell surrounded by a thick, clear capsular halo visible against a dark India Ink background.
